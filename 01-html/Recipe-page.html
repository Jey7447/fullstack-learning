Macaroni Bolognese

How to make macaroni bolognese

Ingredients

  • Macaroni
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Carrot
  • Ground beef
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Add oil, garlic, beef, salt and pepper to the saute pan.
  2. Brown the beef mince.
  3. Add sauce ingredients.
  4. Reserve pasta water.
  5. Simmer sauce for 15 minutes.
  6. Add cooked macaroni.
  7. Add reserved pasta water.
  8. Stir to heat through.
